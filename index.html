Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you deliver in Kuttiady & Kozhikode? +
Standard handcrafted floral bouquets are delivered within 1 day across Kozhikode and Kannur. Arrangements containing fresh Oriental lilies require 3 days, and arrangements containing imported Holland tulips require 4 days for peak freshness conditioning. Morning, evening, and midnight surprise delivery slots are available.
How do I care for flowers in signature velvet boxes? +
Our luxury velvet boxes are fitted with internal moisture-locking floral oasis foam. You do not need to take the flowers out or put them in a vase. Simply pour half a cup of cold water into the center of the arrangement every 2 days and keep in a cool, air-conditioned room.
Can I customize the colors, stems, and message card? +
Yes! You can use our interactive Custom Bouquet Studio right here on the website or message us directly on WhatsApp with your preferred flower varieties, velvet box color, and custom handwritten message. Every order includes a complimentary gold wax-sealed envelope.
How can I place an order directly on WhatsApp / DM? +
Click any of the 'DM for Orders' or 'WhatsApp Order' buttons throughout the site. Your chosen arrangement, delivery slot, and address details will automatically be structured in WhatsApp, allowing for seamless confirmation with our concierge.
